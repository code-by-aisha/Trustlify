# 2. PAKISTAN DIGITAL ENVIRONMENT

DataReportal Digital 2026: Pakistan reports:
- 117 million internet users in October 2025
- 79.9 million social media user identities in October 2025
- 68.4% of Pakistan's internet user base used at least one social media platform

Important caveat:
“social media user identities” are not necessarily unique individuals.
Do not convert these numbers into unsupported behavior claims.

Source:
https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-pakistan

# 5. CURRENT COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

There are already Pakistani platforms focused on opportunity discovery, career development, or personalized recommendations.

Examples include:
- Prime Minister's Digital Youth Hub
- Opportunity.pk
- Career Next Pakistan
- TheOpportunity.pk / Opportunities.com.pk
- other opportunity/career platforms

This is important.

Trustlify should NOT say:
“No one helps Pakistani students find opportunities.”

That would be inaccurate.

Instead:
> “Opportunity discovery already exists. Trustlify focuses on the investigation and decision layer between seeing an opportunity and acting on it.”

---

# 6. COMPETITIVE DIFFERENTIATION

Discovery platforms:
Find / list / recommend opportunities.

Trustlify:
Investigates an opportunity or claim a user has already encountered.

Trustlify additionally combines:
- evidence provenance
- multi-source comparison
- claim-level verification
- currentness
- risk
- source identity
- personal fit
- uncertainty/abstention
- monitoring

---

# 7. RESEARCH WARNING

Search results, community comments and AI-generated summaries are not automatically facts.

For every external claim in the pitch:
- save source
- save date
- save exact statement
- save limitation
- use the source's actual measured population
