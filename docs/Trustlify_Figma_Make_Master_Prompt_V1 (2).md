# TRUSTLIFY — FIGMA MAKE MASTER PROMPT V1
## Full high-fidelity website + product prototype

## 0. INSTRUCTION

Create a complete, high-fidelity, clickable prototype for a product called **Trustlify**.

Trustlify is an evidence-driven AI investigation platform that helps people investigate online opportunities, links, posts, screenshots, PDFs and claims before they click, apply, pay, or share personal information.

Primary persona:
Pakistani student.

Secondary persona:
General user.

The prototype must be:
- premium
- original
- dark
- editorial
- investigative
- technically credible
- visually memorable
- responsive
- coherent across marketing and product screens

This is NOT a generic AI SaaS dashboard.

Do NOT use:
- generic blue/cyan AI gradients
- robot/brain illustrations
- excessive glassmorphism
- generic dashboard cards
- stock photography
- crypto/cyberpunk styling
- noisy neon everywhere
- fake data claims
- unsupported statistics

Use realistic demo content, but do not present fictional sources as real evidence.
